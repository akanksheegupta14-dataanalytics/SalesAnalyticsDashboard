# SalesAnalyticsDashboard
This is an interactive Excel sales dashboard that gives a complete view of order performance and customer behavior at a glance. It tracks key metrics like total orders, revenue, quantitysold , average rating, and delivery time , with filters for customer gender and other channel ( App , Instagram, Partner App, Target.com, Website).

The Dashboard Visualizes :
** Sales trends **  over last 13 weeks (quantity and revenue)
** Purchase channel breakdown ** by gender (App, Instagram, Partner App, Target.com, Website )
** Order size distribution ** - how manyitems customers typically buy
** Shipping performance ** - days taken to deliver orders
** Product popularity ** by category (shorts , dresses, tote bags , sweatshirts, etc.)
** Customer geographic distribution ** (mapped by region )
** Customer satisfaction trends ** by month

Built for quick , filterable insights into what's selling, who's buying, and how well orders are being fullfilled .
